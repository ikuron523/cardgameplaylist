# What is Sevens (Fan Tan)?
I don't know why this game is called "Fan Tan" in some areas, especially in the US. The interesting fact is that the name "Fan Tan" is the name of Chinese gambling game which is very different from this game.

This game "Sevens" is based on a Japanese card game which is usually played just for fun (not for betting). I hope you enjoy this game!

## Basic Rules
- In this game, we use 1 deck of 52 cards without Jokers with 4 players, 3 of them are CPUs and 1 is you. Each player has 13 cards at the beginning.
- First of all, players who has 7 in their hands can play the card(s). The player who has the 7 of diamonds becomes the first to play. After that, each player plays in clockwise direction.
- You can play a card which is continuous from 7, either higher or lower with the same suit.
- If you don't have any cards to play, you must pass, but you can also strategically pass even if you have cards to play. You can pass 3 times at the most. When you pass 4 times (you cannot choose the 4th pass if you have any cards to play), you will be eliminated from the game, and all your remaining cards will be put on the table.

## Winning
- The purpose of the game is either to be the first to empty your hand or eliminate all the other players.

## Operations
- When it's your turn, you can click a card which is click-enabled, or click the PASS button.
- You cannot click the PASS button if you have already passed 3 times and you have any cards to play.
