# Solitaire how to play

## Solitaire
This solitaire game is a standard 3 turn Klondike.

## Basic Rules
- We use a standard 52 card deck.
- The object is to move all cards to the foundation piles.
- The foundation piles are built up in ascending order by suit, starting with Ace.
- In the tableau piles, cards are built down in alternating colors.
- A King, or a pile of cards starting with a King, can be moved to an empty tableau pile.

## Operations
- Click the deck to draw 3 cards to the waste pile.
- You can drag and drop the following cards: (1) the top of the waste pile. (2) any sequence of cards on a pile facing up. (3) the top of a foundation pile.
- If the card at the top of the waste pile or tableau pile can be moved to a foundation pile, you can double-click it to move it.
